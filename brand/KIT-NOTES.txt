USE
Gradient and white variants are intended for dark surfaces; black lettering is for light surfaces.
SVG artwork is transparent unless the filename says primary-dark, primary-light or app-tile.
App tile has a full square black background; platform applies its own corner mask.
Keep the approved framed icon intact for the main app identity.
Unframed microphone symbol is a secondary product/avatar mark.
The 16px and 24px exports are supplied for compatibility; the detailed icon reads better from 32px.
Do not stretch artwork, change relative spacing or add effects.
Suggested clear space: at least 1/8 of icon width. This is a working brand recommendation, not a platform requirement.
Small printed use, embroidery, engraving and process-color printing need supplier proofing.
Use the one-color SVG for single-ink or etched production; gradients are RGB digital artwork.
