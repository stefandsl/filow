# Filow 0.9.45 internal beta

## Speaking and presenting

- Background listening uses less CPU. On the test PC it used less than one CPU core.
- When the microphone or phone relay disconnects, Filow pauses the take. Recover your place and see which points you covered on both the desktop and the phone. Filow also handles spoken corrections and points you reopen by hand.
- Phone pairing starts on your local network. To pair from another network, turn on the public link and confirm. You can close it while keeping the local session open.
- The welcome tour shows the available features, with narration and captions in each supported language. The first line of the Russian narration is clearer. Choose **Talk or type** to try a practice question, including when the microphone is unavailable.
- The OK Filow bubble stays still when you approach it. Press Esc to dismiss it while keeping keyboard focus in your current app. The report window lets you clear diagnostic history after confirming.

## Whiteboard and daily tasks

- Messages travel along their arrows to the receiving box. Move a box and its arrow labels follow. Arrows, circles and highlights added in later steps use the new position too.
- Ask for a cycle or a comparison on the whiteboard. In the prepared water-cycle lesson, a learner can change the air temperature in Cloud Lab and see what happens. Choose **Return to my point** to resume your presentation.
- In **Day-by-day**, add tasks for today or tomorrow. Review a spoken or typed progress report before marking a task complete. Checking spoken reports requires permission and an installed local model. You can also tick tasks by hand. Completed tasks stay in the archive for 30 days and can be restored.

## Desktop updates

- Right-click the OK Filow tray icon and choose **Quit** to close Filow completely.
- The Windows tray icon is clearer at small sizes, and screen readers announce startup progress. Phone pairing gives more specific help when Windows Firewall blocks a connection.
- Before installing a downloaded update, Filow checks that the version is still available. A withdrawn update stays off your computer.

## Internal testing

The team now checks speech changes against the same English and Italian examples, including how Filow matches spoken words to their meaning. This build excludes the computer-use pilots and hides their setting.
